Privacy

The website does not require an account. Crossword progress is stored in your browser's localStorage on your device. You can clear it using your browser's site-data settings.

We have not configured advertising or analytics scripts in this initial release. Hosting infrastructure may process technical request data under its own policies.

This policy describes the website only, not the Android application. It will be reviewed before app links or additional services are added.
